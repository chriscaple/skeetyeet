Privacy

Draft. Last updated November 11, 2026.

SkeetYeet is made by Carmel Avenue Media. The short version: we don't collect anything.

What SkeetYeet collects

Nothing. SkeetYeet has no server and no user accounts. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What happens on your device

When you connect a Bluesky account, SkeetYeet talks directly to your Bluesky server (PDS) and Bluesky's public services. Your sign-in tokens are stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain, Android Keystore or Windows Credential Manager). Your post index, settings and archives are stored on your device, in a location you choose for archives. Nothing is sent to Carmel Avenue Media.

Tips

If you leave a tip, the payment is handled by Apple, Google or GitHub Sponsors under their own privacy policies. SkeetYeet stores no payment information.

This website

skeetyeet.app is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub or contact Carmel Avenue Media.

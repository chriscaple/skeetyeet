How it works

  1. Connect your Bluesky account.
  2. Filter to pick what goes.
  3. Preview every item, and spare any you like.
  4. Archive a copy to your device.
  5. Let go. Then go outside.

Your stuff stays yours

SkeetYeet has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It talks only to your own Bluesky server, and your archive lives on your device. Every line of code is on GitHub.

Questions

Why do big cleanups take a while?

Bluesky limits how many changes an account can make per hour (roughly 5,000 deletions an hour on bsky.social). SkeetYeet works within those limits and picks up where it left off, so a very large account may take a day or two. Perfect time to go to the beach.

Is deleted really gone?

SkeetYeet deletes posts from your account and Bluesky stops showing them. Copies others already made (screenshots, quotes, third-party archives, services that recorded the public firehose) are outside anyone's control.

How do I sign in?

With Bluesky's own sign-in page (OAuth), so SkeetYeet never sees your password. If your server doesn't support that yet, you can use an app password. Never your main password.

Can I delete my DMs?

You can export them. Bluesky only lets you delete messages from your own side of a conversation.

Is SkeetYeet made by Bluesky?

No. SkeetYeet is an independent, open-source project by Carmel Avenue Media and is not affiliated with Bluesky Social PBC.
